# Preface: What This Book Is

Trading books love detail. Entry conditions, indicator settings, backtest parameters, risk rules, documented results. Then the page turns. What almost never follows is the question that actually matters: why does this strategy work, whose money is on the other side of it, and why do they keep losing that money after thousands of readers have learned the same published rules? Those questions sit at the center of every chapter here. The book is a concentrated study of trading edges, assembled entirely from the public record: published books, public video instruction, and peer-reviewed financial research. No proprietary sources. No secrets extracted. Instead, it reconstructs what the public evidence implies but does not state, and every reconstruction is clearly marked as conjecture, separated from the source material it interprets, aimed at the strategy archetype rather than at any individual's private intent. You read the sourced fact, and then you read what the sourced fact implies.

## What the book covers

Every edge has a source. The book sorts them by what that source is. Part I, *The Build*, covers edges rooted in private knowledge and in quantitative models. Knowledge edges resist copying because they depend on what you personally know, observe, or have lived through. Model edges are different. They decay fastest after publication, because replication begins the moment the paper appears in print.

Part II, *The Behavioral*, sorts five strategy families by the psychological flaw each one exploits: breakout patterns, trend following, mean reversion, relative strength, and event-driven catalysts. The biases behind them are hardwired, which is why the edges keep regenerating. That is not the same as saying they never erode.

Part III, *The Structural*, shifts the explanation from psychology to plumbing. Forced flows, carry, the volatility risk premium, and microstructure all generate returns because institutions have no choice but to participate on the losing side. The counterparty here is not making a behavioral mistake. The counterparty is obeying a mandate that leaves no room for discretion.

Then risk management. Then the question every trader eventually faces: which edges survive?

## How each case is presented

Two layers. Visibly separated. The first is a faithful rendering of what the public source described: the strategy, the parameters, the documented outcomes. Read that layer as reported fact and nothing more.

The second is marked *Reconstruction* and contains the book's own conjecture about the underlying mechanism. This book is written by an AI system rather than a human analyst, which means that conjecture is machine inference: analysis applied to the public record, not reasoning any source stated. Weigh it as conjecture from that source, checked against the faithful layer above it and the cited evidence. In that layer the book argues, tests, and proposes explanations the source did not state. A guessed edge is never presented as a settled conclusion.

Evidence quality is noted for every case: backtested, tested out of sample, live traded, asserted by the author, or self-reported without documentation, in descending order of weight. Cases built on rigorous backtests or verified live performance carry more analytical weight than those resting on assertion alone. You will notice the difference in how the book handles them.

Each case also carries a durability label signaling how the edge has held up over time. Chapter 1 defines the full classification. One label worth noting here: *cautionary* means the method is sound but the implementation is unproven or unvalidated.

Published book authors who discuss their own methods are named and cited normally. Individual practitioners drawn from video sources are anonymized. No non-public information appears anywhere.

## What the book does not promise

A manual tells you what to trade tomorrow. This is not that. It traces the edges that exist in the public record, documents which ones have decayed and which ones persist, and leaves you with a framework for telling a real edge from a flattering story.

One claim runs through every chapter: every edge has a lifecycle. Discovered, exploited, crowded, compressed. The only lasting response is adaptation and the willingness to let go of edges that have died.
